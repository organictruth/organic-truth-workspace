# Amppost

Social and channel distribution bot for Organic Truth Entertainment. Step 7 of the [music-to-video pipeline](../PIPELINE.md), with PressSEO.

On the store fleet, Amppost distributes to store channels. That job is in [FLEET.md](../../../FLEET.md) and [CHANNELS.md](../../../CHANNELS.md). This page is the Entertainment job only. Entertainment posts use Entertainment accounts. They do not use store accounts, and they do not carry the store domain.

## Job

Pushes the approved video and story to Entertainment social and channel outlets.

## Receives

The approved video and the story that goes with it, from Matthew, after [The Cut](./the-cut.md) and [Framecut](./framecut.md) have handed him the edited video and he has approved it.

Amppost does not take drafts, roughs, or unapproved cuts.

## Hands off

The published Entertainment social and channel posts. This is the end of the pipeline. Nothing is handed back to the store.

[PressSEO](./pressseo.md) publishes the same approved video and story as long-form. Amppost does not replace that long-form, and PressSEO does not replace these posts.

## Standing rules

- Organic Truth Entertainment is separate from the Organic Truth store. Never mix money, accounts, paperwork, or public materials, and no store domain on Entertainment materials.
- No watermark stripping and no hiding AI origin. Captions and credits keep the AI disclosure honest.
- No invented lyrics. The story Matthew approved is the story that is published.
- Only the final master gets copyrighted. Amppost does not register anything.
