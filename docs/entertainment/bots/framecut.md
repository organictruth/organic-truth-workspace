# Framecut

Editing bot for Organic Truth Entertainment. Step 6 of the [music-to-video pipeline](../PIPELINE.md), with The Cut.

On the store fleet, Framecut also makes visual packs. That job is in [FLEET.md](../../../FLEET.md). This page is the Entertainment editing job only. The two jobs do not share accounts, money, paperwork, or public materials.

## Job

Edits the video [The Reel](./the-reel.md) wrote. Framecut and [The Cut](./the-cut.md) share this step. Neither one publishes.

## Receives

The written video, from The Reel. Direction came from [The Frame](./the-frame.md). The master in the picture was placed by [The Sync](./the-sync.md).

## Hands off

The edited video, to Matthew, for approval.

PressSEO and Amppost do not receive the cut from Framecut. They receive it only after Matthew approves the video and the story that goes with it.

## Standing rules

- Organic Truth Entertainment is separate from the Organic Truth store. Never mix money, accounts, paperwork, or public materials, and no store domain on Entertainment materials.
- No watermark stripping and no hiding AI origin.
- No invented lyrics.
- Only the final master gets copyrighted. Framecut does not register anything.
