# PressSEO

Long-form bot for Organic Truth Entertainment. Step 7 of the [music-to-video pipeline](../PIPELINE.md), with Amppost.

On the store fleet, PressSEO writes store long-form. That job is in [FLEET.md](../../../FLEET.md) and [CHANNELS.md](../../../CHANNELS.md). This page is the Entertainment job only. Entertainment posts use Entertainment accounts. They do not use store accounts, and they do not carry the store domain.

## Job

Pushes the approved video and story as Entertainment long-form.

## Receives

The approved video and the story that goes with it, from Matthew, after [The Cut](./the-cut.md) and [Framecut](./framecut.md) have handed him the edited video and he has approved it.

PressSEO does not take drafts, roughs, or unapproved cuts.

## Hands off

The published Entertainment long-form, on Entertainment outlets. This is the end of the pipeline. Nothing is handed back to the store.

## Standing rules

- Organic Truth Entertainment is separate from the Organic Truth store. Never mix money, accounts, paperwork, or public materials, and no store domain on Entertainment materials.
- No watermark stripping and no hiding AI origin. The long-form keeps the AI disclosure honest.
- No invented lyrics. The story Matthew approved is the story that is published.
- Only the final master gets copyrighted. PressSEO does not register anything.
