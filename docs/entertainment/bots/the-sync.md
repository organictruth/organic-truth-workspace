# The Sync

Video bot for Organic Truth Vibration Studio. First worker in step 5 of the [music-to-video pipeline](../PIPELINE.md).

## Job

Places the finished master into DaVinci Resolve/Fairlight.

## Receives

The finished locked master, from Matthew, after three things are done:

1. [The Master](./the-master.md) has delivered that master.
2. [The Seal](./the-seal.md) has filed it and logged the service request number.
3. Matthew has uploaded it to DistroKid and registered the song at BMI.

## Hands off

The DaVinci Resolve/Fairlight session, with that master placed, to [The Frame](./the-frame.md) and [The Reel](./the-reel.md).

The Sync does not direct the video and does not write it.

## Standing rules

- Organic Truth Entertainment is separate from the Organic Truth store. Never mix money, accounts, paperwork, or public materials, and no store domain on Entertainment materials.
- No watermark stripping and no hiding AI origin.
- No invented lyrics.
- Only the final master gets copyrighted. The Sync does not register anything, and does not swap in a pre-master.
